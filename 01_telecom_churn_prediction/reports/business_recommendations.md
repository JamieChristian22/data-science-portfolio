# Business Recommendations

## Decision objective
Churn prediction is not primarily an accuracy problem. Retention teams need enough recall to identify a meaningful share of customers likely to leave, while keeping outreach volume and incentive cost manageable.

## Operating approach
1. Score the active base weekly and rank customers by churn probability.
2. Use the validation-selected business threshold for outreach eligibility.
3. Prioritize the highest-risk customers first when campaign capacity is limited.
4. Tailor retention actions to key drivers: contract flexibility, support friction, late-payment behavior, price sensitivity, and satisfaction.
5. Track incremental saves and campaign ROI rather than model accuracy alone.

## KPIs to monitor
- Churn recall and precision
- PR-AUC and ROC-AUC
- Contact rate
- Save rate among contacted churners
- Cost per retained customer
- Net retained value
- Probability calibration and data drift
