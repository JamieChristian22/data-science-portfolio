# Responsible AI, Fair Lending & Model Governance

This portfolio project deliberately separates **technical demonstration** from **real lending use**.

A real credit model would require:
- documented permissible purpose and feature review;
- legal/compliance assessment for fair-lending and consumer-protection obligations;
- testing for disparate impact and proxy variables using appropriately governed data;
- adverse-action reason processes where applicable;
- independent model validation and model-risk-management approval;
- calibration, stability, drift, and outcome monitoring;
- challenger models and periodic revalidation;
- human escalation and exception-management procedures;
- security, privacy, access control, retention, and audit logging.

No protected attributes are included in this synthetic dataset. Their absence does **not** by itself prove fairness.
