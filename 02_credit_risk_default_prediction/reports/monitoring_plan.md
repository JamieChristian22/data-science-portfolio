# Monitoring Plan

## Monthly
- applicant count and approval/review distribution
- score distribution / PSI
- missingness and schema checks
- observed default rate by risk band when outcomes mature
- calibration by decile
- false-negative and false-positive rates

## Quarterly
- ROC-AUC, PR-AUC, KS and Brier score
- feature distribution drift
- segment stability
- challenger comparison
- policy threshold review

## Escalation examples
Investigate when discrimination, calibration, default rate, approval mix, or major feature distributions move outside validated tolerance.
