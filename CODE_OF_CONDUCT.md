# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

This independent data science portfolio welcomes respectful, constructive participation from learners, analysts, researchers, and developers of all backgrounds and experience levels.

## Expected behavior

- Communicate professionally and give specific, actionable feedback.
- Treat other contributors and their perspectives with respect.
- Document analytical assumptions, limitations, uncertainty, and known model risks.
- Respect data privacy, licensing requirements, and responsible AI principles.
- Use synthetic, public, or properly authorized datasets when sharing examples.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, personal attacks, publication of private information without permission, and knowingly misleading claims about model performance or real-world outcomes are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through an available non-public contact channel. Do not post identifying information, private datasets, or sensitive reports in public issues. The maintainer may remove harmful material, close discussions, or limit participation as necessary. No guaranteed response time is promised.

## Scope

This code applies to repository issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-related community interactions.
